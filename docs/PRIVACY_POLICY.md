# Hew7 Privacy Policy

**Effective: 25 October 2026 · Hew7 Studio**

Hew7 is a launcher for Android TV and Google TV. This policy explains what Hew7
does with information. In short: Hew7 has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and Hew7
Studio receives no information about you.

## What stays on your TV

These are stored only on the TV, in Hew7's private storage, and are deleted when Hew7 is
uninstalled:

- Your Hew7 settings (theme, font, app order, wallpaper and screensaver choices, remote button
  choices).
- The last weather forecast and approximate location, so the screen isn't empty while offline.
- The current wallpaper's address.

Hew7 reads some information from the TV only to show it to you, and never sends it anywhere:
the list of installed apps (for the app dock), CPU, memory, storage and network figures (Machine
Room), running apps and their memory use (Running Apps, if you grant access), and the Wi-Fi
network's name (if you grant location access).

## What is sent over the internet

To show the weather and wallpapers, Hew7 contacts these services directly from the TV. They
receive your TV's internet (IP) address, as any website does:

| Service | Purpose | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| ipwho.is, ipapi.co | Find your approximate location (city) for the weather | Your IP address |
| Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) | Weather forecast | Approximate latitude and longitude (city level) |
| Bing (bing.com) | Daily wallpaper images | Your IP address |
| Lorem Picsum (picsum.photos) | Photo wallpapers | Your IP address |

Hew7 doesn't send your name, accounts, viewing activity, app list or any identifier. Each
service's own privacy policy applies to the requests it receives.

## Hew7 Helper (Accessibility service)

Hew7 Helper is optional and off until you switch it on in the TV's Accessibility settings, after
Hew7 explains what it does. It uses Android's Accessibility API only to:

- press Force Stop for apps you choose to close in Running Apps;
- open the TV's Display and Sound, Accounts and power menu screens and press the option you chose
  (Restart, Shut Down);
- see which remote button you press (Settings, Input, Menu, app buttons) while Hew7's home screen
  is in front, or app buttons from anywhere, so they open what you set.

It reads only the TV's settings screens and power menu while carrying out these actions. It does
not read the content of other apps, and it collects, stores and shares nothing.

## Children

Hew7 isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective
